## 1.0.0

- First release: Basic panel dials (Temp, Tint, Exposure, Contrast, Highlights, Shadows, Whites,
  Blacks, Texture, Clarity, Dehaze, Vibrance, Saturation) and a Develop Slider dial for every
  other panel (tone curve, HSL, color grading, detail, lens corrections, transform, effects,
  calibration), with live values and press to reset; modules, next / previous photo, flag,
  rating (key and dial), colour label, rotate, copy / paste settings, reset all, auto tone and
  auto white balance, before / after, Develop tools and panels, undo / redo. Through the KnobDeck
  plug-in for Lightroom Classic, which setup installs.
