Discord Setup

Keep the Discord desktop app open and signed in. Discord shows an authorization popup in the app when you continue.

Discord limits who can authorize an app. Discord lets an app use RPC (the local connection KnobDeck uses to control Discord) only after Discord approves it. Until KnobDeck's Discord app is approved, only a short list of test accounts can authorize it, and setup fails for everyone else with invalid_scope. This is a restriction on Discord's side. To use the plugin now, create your own Discord app (free, about two minutes) and enter its Client ID below.
How to create your own Discord app
  1. Open https://discord.com/developers/applications in your browser. Sign in with the account you use in the Discord app, then click New Application and give it any name.
  2. Open OAuth2 and copy the Client ID.
  3. On the same page, add http://localhost under Redirects, turn on Public Client, and click Save Changes.
  4. Paste the Client ID below. KnobDeck doesn't need the client secret.
KnobDeck saves it with your Discord sign-in in your system's credential store (Keychain, Windows Credential Manager or Secret Service).
