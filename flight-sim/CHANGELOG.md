# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release: Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 and 2024 over SimConnect. Autopilot master and
  modes, heading / altitude / vertical speed / speed / course dials, COM and NAV standby
  frequency dials and swap, transponder, gear, flaps, spoilers, parking brake, lights,
  battery / alternator / avionics, trim, altimeter, pause, sim rate, flight readouts, and any
  K-event or SimVar for power users. Every key and dial shows the sim's live state.
