## 1.0.0

- Speak Text: the selection, the clipboard or your own text (KnobDeck placeholders), with a voice
  per key; pressing while speaking stops it or speaks the new text.
- Stop Speaking (lit while speaking), Use Voice (a voice picker), Speech Rate and Speech Volume
  dials; voice, rate and volume are kept.
- Windows: WinRT SpeechSynthesizer. macOS: the system synthesizer through `say`. Linux: Speech
  Dispatcher over SSIP (started when it isn't running).
